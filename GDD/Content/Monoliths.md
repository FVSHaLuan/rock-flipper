## Creative Guidance
- Player can unlock monoliths at any order, so their abilities must feel unique, and not comparable.
- A monolith's effect does not need to be visually traceable back to it.
- A monolith should feel like a powerful unlock, like unlocking a whole new system with many related upgrades.
