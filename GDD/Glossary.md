# Glossary

Game-specific terms, in alphabetical order. Each entry is a short definition; the linked doc is the source of truth.

* **Active background**: the unlocked background the player has set as active. → [Backgrounds](Systems/Backgrounds.md#unlocking)
* **Active state**: the Flipper Bot state in which it moves and flips. → [Flipper Bots](Systems/FlipperBots.md#active-state)
* **Background**: the bottom layer of the playfield, representing the current biome. → [Main Screen](Screens/MainScreen.md#background), [Backgrounds](Systems/Backgrounds.md)
* **Background elements**: decorations representing the biome, scattered over the playfield. → [Main Screen](Screens/MainScreen.md#background)
* **Biome**: the environment of the playfield (e.g. grassland, desert), shown by the background; a development-only term not shown to players. → [Main Screen](Screens/MainScreen.md#background)
* **Bounce**: a Bouncy rock landing once more after a landing. → [Rock Tiers](Content/Rock%20Tiers.md#p1-bouncy)
* **Bounce chance**: the chance that a Bouncy rock bounces after landing. → [Rock Tiers](Content/Rock%20Tiers.md#p1-bouncy)
* **Bouncy**: the P1 rock tier. → [Rock Tiers](Content/Rock%20Tiers.md#p1-bouncy)
* **Breaking**: what happens to a rock or chest when its HP reaches 0. → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md#breaking), [Chests](Systems/Chests.md#flipping--breaking)
* **Breaking cash**: cash earned when a rock breaks. → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md#breaking)
* **Breaking cash multiplier**: the tier-specific stat that sets breaking cash as a multiple of landing cash. → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md#tiers)
* **Cash** (`$`): the currency spent on most upgrades. → [Currencies](Systems/Currencies.md)
* **Chest**: an object dropped when the chest bar fills, opened to drop items. → [Chests](Systems/Chests.md)
* **Chest bar**: the overlay UI bar that drops a chest when full. → [Chests](Systems/Chests.md#chest-bar)
* **Chest exp**: what fills the chest bar. → [Chests](Systems/Chests.md#chest-bar)
* **Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Unique**: the rarities of chests and items. → [Chest Stats](Stats/Chest%20Stats.md)
* **Critical cash multiplier**: the multiplier applied to landing cash on a critical landing. → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md#critical-landing)
* **Critical landing**: a landing that earns multiplied landing cash. → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md#critical-landing)
* **Critical landing chance**: the chance that a landing is a critical landing. → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md#critical-landing)
* **Currency**: something spent on upgrades (cash or stars). → [Currencies](Systems/Currencies.md)
* **Flipper Bot**: a floating robot that flips rocks. → [Flipper Bots](Systems/FlipperBots.md)
* **Flipping**: throwing a rock from the ground up into the air. → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md#flipping)
* **Gameplay elements**: the middle layer of the playfield (rocks, Flipper Bots, monoliths,...). → [Main Screen](Screens/MainScreen.md#gameplay-elements)
* **HP**: an integer stat of rocks and chests. → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md#hp)
* **Hover ability**: the unlockable ability to flip rocks by hovering the mouse over them. → [Mouse](Systems/Mouse.md#hover)
* **Inactive state**: the Flipper Bot state in which it doesn't move or flip. → [Flipper Bots](Systems/FlipperBots.md#inactive-state)
* **Item**: something dropped from opened chests. → [Items](Systems/Items.md)
* **Item effect**: an item's effect, scaling with its item level. → [Items](Systems/Items.md#item-effect)
* **Item exp**: what fills an item's item level bar. → [Items](Systems/Items.md#item-progression)
* **Item level / Item level bar**: an item's level, raised by filling its item level bar. → [Items](Systems/Items.md#item-progression)
* **Landing**: a flipped rock coming back down. → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md#flipping)
* **Landing cash**: cash earned each time a rock lands. → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md#flipping)
* **Level / Leveling up**: the player's level, raised by filling the level bar. → [Levels](Systems/Levels.md)
* **Level bar**: the overlay UI bar that levels the player up when full. → [Levels](Systems/Levels.md#level-bar)
* **Level exp**: what fills the level bar. → [Levels](Systems/Levels.md#level-bar)
* **Max count**: the maximum number of rocks of a tier, or of Flipper Bots. → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md#tiers), [Flipper Bots](Systems/FlipperBots.md)
* **Max HP**: the HP a rock starts with (per tier); also a chest stat (per rarity). → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md#tiers), [Chest Stats](Stats/Chest%20Stats.md)
* **Monolith**: a floating structure at the center of the playfield. → [Monoliths](Systems/Monoliths.md)
* **Monolith ability**: a monolith's unique ability. → [Monoliths](Systems/Monoliths.md#ability)
* **Mouse**: the player's mouse cursor, used to flip rocks. → [Mouse](Systems/Mouse.md)
* **Mouse radius**: the unlockable ability to flip all rocks within a radius around the mouse cursor at once. → [Mouse](Systems/Mouse.md#mouse-radius)
* **Node**: an element of the Skill Tree; each node is a skill. → [Skill Tree](Systems/SkillTree.md#nodes)
* **Opening (a chest)**: getting a chest's contents out. → [Chests](Systems/Chests.md#opening)
* **Overlay UI elements**: the top layer of the playfield (Current cash, Chest bar, Level bar). → [Main Screen](Screens/MainScreen.md#overlay-ui-elements)
* **P0, P1, P2,...**: rock tier names. → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md#tiers)
* **Parent skill**: a skill that another skill depends on. → [Skill Tree](Systems/SkillTree.md#structure)
* **Playfield**: the left 3/4 of the main screen, where all the gameplay happens. → [Main Screen](Screens/MainScreen.md#playfield)
* **Preferred background**: the unlocked background currently marked Preferred. → [Backgrounds](Systems/Backgrounds.md#preferred-background)
* **Preferred Interval**: how often the Preferred background changes. → [Backgrounds](Systems/Backgrounds.md#preferred-background)
* **Preferred Multiplier**: the cash income multiplier for using the Preferred background as the active background. → [Backgrounds](Systems/Backgrounds.md#preferred-background)
* **Pure / Purity**: a pure rock earns a multiplied cash income. → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md#purity)
* **Rarity**: a chest or item category (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Unique). → [Chests](Systems/Chests.md#rarity), [Items](Systems/Items.md#rarity)
* **Restless**: the P2 rock tier. → [Rock Tiers](Content/Rock%20Tiers.md#p2-restless)
* **Rock**: an object lying around the playfield, flipped to earn cash. → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md)
* **Root skill**: the only skill with no parent skills. → [Skill Tree](Systems/SkillTree.md#structure)
* **Self-flip**: a Restless rock flipping itself. → [Rock Tiers](Content/Rock%20Tiers.md#p2-restless)
* **Self-flip chance**: the chance that a Restless rock self-flips once its self-flip delay is reached. → [Rock Tiers](Content/Rock%20Tiers.md#p2-restless)
* **Self-flip delay**: how long a Restless rock stays on the ground before it may self-flip. → [Rock Tiers](Content/Rock%20Tiers.md#p2-restless)
* **Side bar**: the right 1/4 of the main screen, a column of buttons. → [Main Screen](Screens/MainScreen.md#side-bar)
* **Skill**: a Skill Tree node, which unlocks or upgrades things. → [Skill Tree](Systems/SkillTree.md#nodes)
* **Skill level**: the level of a skill. → [Skill Tree](Systems/SkillTree.md#nodes)
* **Skill Tree**: the system of nodes (skills) where most of the game's progression happens. → [Skill Tree](Systems/SkillTree.md)
* **System Menu**: a screen opened from the side bar. *Not yet documented.* → [Main Screen](Screens/MainScreen.md#side-bar)
* **Star** (`ST`): the hard-to-earn currency, spent on a few rare upgrades. → [Currencies](Systems/Currencies.md)
* **The Rift**: a black-hole-like circle at the center of the playfield. *WIP.* → [The Rift](Systems/TheRift.md)
* **Tier**: a rock category (P0, P1, P2,...). → [Rocks](Systems/Rocks.md#tiers)
* **Upgrade**: the general act of spending currencies to progress the game. → [Pillars](Pillars.md#core-loop)
