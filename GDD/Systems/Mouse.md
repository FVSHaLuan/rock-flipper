# Mouse

The player's **mouse** cursor, used to [flip](Rocks.md#flipping) rocks. From the start of the game, the player must click on rocks to flip them.

## Purpose

## Hover
After the **hover ability** is unlocked, the player only needs to hover the mouse over rocks to flip them.

## Mouse Radius
After the **mouse radius** is unlocked, all rocks within a radius around the mouse cursor are flipped at once, instead of only the rock directly under the cursor. The mouse radius size is listed in [Mouse Stats](../Stats/Mouse%20Stats.md).
