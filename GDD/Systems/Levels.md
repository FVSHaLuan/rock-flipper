# Levels

The player **levels up** by filling the **level bar**, earning a [star](Currencies.md).

## Purpose

## Level Bar
* When a [rock](Rocks.md) lands, it adds **level exp** to the level bar (see [Main Screen](../Screens/MainScreen.md#overlay-ui-elements) for its position). Level exp per landing is a [rock stat](../Stats/Rock%20Stats.md).
* When the level bar fills up, the player earns exactly 1 star (this amount can't be upgraded), and the bar resets and becomes harder to fill.
