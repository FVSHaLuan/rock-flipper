# Temporary Stats

**Temporary stats** are stats of an individual [rock](Rocks.md) that reset every time it flips. All temporary stats are listed in [Temporary Stats](../Stats/Temporary%20Stats.md).

## Purpose

## Rules
* Each rock has its own temporary stats.
* Whenever a rock starts to flip, its temporary stats reset to their default values.
* Temporary stats can only be modified while the rock is airborne, which can happen in several ways (e.g. [L VIII](../Content/Monoliths.md#l-viii) beams increase the temporary multiplier).

## Temporary Multiplier
The **temporary multiplier** has a default value of 1. On landing, the cash the rock earns is multiplied by its temporary multiplier, including when the landing breaks the rock.

## Temporary Critical Landing Chance
The **temporary critical landing chance** has a default value of 0. On landing, it increases the [critical landing chance](Rocks.md#critical-landing).

## Temporary Pure Chance
The **temporary pure chance** has a default value of 0. Like the temporary critical landing chance, on landing it increases the chance to be [pure](Rocks.md#purity).
