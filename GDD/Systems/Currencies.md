# Currencies

The game has two **currencies**, cash and star, both spent on [upgrades](../Pillars.md#core-loop).

## Purpose

## Currency Types
| Currency | Symbol | Earned from | Spent on |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cash** | `$`, written like a real-life currency, e.g. $500 | Mostly flipping [rocks](Rocks.md) and opening [chests](Chests.md#opening) (via the [items](Items.md) found) | Most upgrades |
| **Star** | `ST` prefix, e.g. ST275 | Only [leveling up](Levels.md), 1 star per level-up | A few rare upgrades |

Stars are designed to be hard to earn. Leveling up is the only way to earn stars, and the amount of 1 star per level-up can't be upgraded.

All different cash multipliers (e.g. [purity](Rocks.md#purity), [critical cash multiplier](Rocks.md#critical-landing), [Preferred Multiplier](Backgrounds.md#preferred-background)) are multiplied together.
