# Rock Flipper - Pillars

## Metadata
* **Title**: Rock Flipper
* **Monetization**: one time purchase (upfront)
* **Genres**: Incremental, idle, casual, indie, single player
* **Store**: Steam
* **Intended device**: PC with mouse and keyboard
* **Art style**: 2D, minimalistic, float
* **Target play time**: 5-7 hours
* **Offline progress**: none; nothing progresses while the game is closed

## Vibe
* **World setting**: the game doesn't strongly belong to a single category like sci-fi, fantasy, or modern,.... It can have elements from any of those category, but sci-fi and modern should be prominent.

* **Theme**: mining, rocks, mineral, money, capitalism, sci-fi, fantasy

* **Visual satisfaction**: looking at rocks flying up and falling down. Slowly adding many stuff to the screen and watching them working together and earning cash.

* **Gameplay satisfaction**: frequently getting new upgrades, unlock new mechanics, systems. Completing (maxing out) everythings.

* **Incremental**: every stats just increase overtime with minimal effort from the player. Players don't need muscular/reflective/reflexive skills or strategic thinking, just play along from the start to the end of the game and watch the game's mechanics, systems, stories,...to unfold

* **Writing Tone**: keep everything light and casual, a little humour here and there

## Core Mechanics
The game revolves around 2 core mechanics:
- **[Rock Flipping](./Systems/Rocks.md)**: throwing rocks up into the air and earning cash when they land.
- **[Skill Tree](Systems/SkillTree.md)**: most of the game upgrade, mechanic unlocking happen via the skill tree

## Core Loop
Flip the rocks -> earn cash -> buy upgrades

An **upgrade** is the general act of spending currencies to progress the game, e.g. leveling up a [skill](Systems/SkillTree.md#nodes), or buying more rocks or Flipper Bots.

There's no sessions, everythings are just on the playfield, in the early game you must interact but later they can work on their owns.

You can buy any upgrades as long as they are unlocked and you have enough currencies.

The game ends when there's no upgrades left to buy and all items are at their max levels.
