# Rock Flipper's GDD document - Vibe

* **Central concept**: the game revolves around throwing rocks up into the air and earning cash when they land.

* **World setting**: the game doesn't strongly belong to a single category like sci-fi, fantasy, or modern,.... It can have elements from any of those category, but sci-fi and modern should be prominent.

* **Theme**: mining, rocks, mineral, money, capitalism, sci-fi, fantasy

* **Visual satisfaction**: looking at rocks flying up and falling down. Slowly adding many stuff to the screen and watching them working together and earning cash.

* **Gameplay satisfaction**: frequently getting new upgrades, unlock new mechanics, systems. Completing (maxing out) everythings.

* **Incremental**: every stats just increase overtime with minimal effort from the player. Players don't need muscular/reflective/reflexive skills or strategic thinking, just play along from the start to the end of the game and watch the game's mechanics, systems, stories,...to unfold

* **Writing Tone**: keep everything light and casual, a little humour here and there
