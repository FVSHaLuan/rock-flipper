# Rock Flipper's GDD - Backgrounds

Unlock in Skill Tree by spending Star currency.

# Preferred Background
- When there's at least 2 backgrounds available, at anytime there'll be a background marked as **Preferred**. 
- The Preferred background changes every **Preferred Interval** (TBD). The new Preferred background is picked randomly from the other available backgrounds, so it always changes to a different one.
- Player sets the Preferred background as the active background to enjoy an income multiplier, **Preferred Multiplier**.
- Preferred Multiplier increases as player unlocks more background.

# Unique Items
Some items can only be found in specific backgrounds.
