# Rock Flipper's GDD - Currencies

* Cash: 
  * Symbol: $, write it like real-life currency, e.g: $500
  * Earning sources:mostly from flipping rocks and openning chests
  * Usage: buy most upgrades
* Star:
  * Symbol: ST, e.g: ST275
  * Earning sources: from leveling up
  * Usage: buy a few rare upgrades, and so stars are designed to hard to earn (see [Levels](./Levels.md))
